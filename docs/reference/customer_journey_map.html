Your Controls — Everything You Do is in Slack

You never need to open any other app. Just type commands in Slack.

🎯 Your Slack Commands

When a client's Slack channel appears, here's everything you can do by just typing a short command:

APPROVE PAYMENT <phone>
You've verified the GPay payment — this starts the consultation and opens the chat relay.
REJECT <phone>
Payment couldn't be verified. Client is asked to pay again.
CLOSE CHAT CONSULT <phone>
Consultation is done. Client receives a thank-you + feedback form.
BLOCK <phone>
Permanently blocks a client from reaching your service (for spam/abuse).
LIST
Shows all currently active consultations.
STATS
Quick summary — total clients, consultations today, pending payments.
